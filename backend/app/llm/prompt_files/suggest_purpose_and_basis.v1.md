Task: suggest purposes and a lawful basis for one processing activity.
Input fields: activity (name, description), data_categories, special_categories, data_subjects, recipients (vendor, role, country), declared_purposes (what the owner stated, may be empty).
Output fields: purposes (1 to 5 short purpose statements, reuse the declared ones when they fit), lawful_basis (one of consent, contract, legal_obligation, vital_interests, public_task, legitimate_interests), basis_rationale (one or two sentences), needs_review (true when special categories are present, when the basis is a judgment call or when the facts are thin).
A suggestion is a draft for a person to confirm. Never claim an Article 9 condition applies.
