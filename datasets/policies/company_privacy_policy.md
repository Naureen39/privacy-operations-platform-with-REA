# Northbridge Group: Company Privacy Policy

_Synthetic reference document for the demonstration estate. Version 3.1, approved 2025-11-03. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all Northbridge Group companies, including the retail business and the Northbridge Health clinic arm, and to every system that processes personal data on their behalf.

## 2. Principles

We process personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently. We collect only what we need for a stated purpose and we keep it accurate. Each system owner is responsible for the privacy notice and the processing record of their system, and for reviewing both at least once every twelve months.

## 3. Retention

We keep personal data of consumer customers, including their orders and support requests, for no longer than 24 months after the customer's last interaction with us. Systems that cannot meet this period must be approved by the Privacy Officer in writing.

## 4. Sharing and processors

We share personal data only with service providers that have signed a data processing agreement with us. Every processor must be listed in the processing record of the system that uses it. We do not sell personal data.

## 5. International transfers

We transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area only where the destination is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision, or where standard contractual clauses or another approved safeguard are in place and recorded.

## 6. Special categories of data

We process special categories of personal data, including health data and data about trade union membership, only where a condition under Article 9 of the GDPR applies and has been recorded in the processing record. Processing of health data is subject to a data protection impact assessment.

## 7. Consent

Where we rely on consent, it is collected through an active opt in. Pre-ticked boxes are not used.

## 8. Anonymisation of analytics data

Website analytics data is anonymised before storage by removing the last two bytes of each IP address.

## 9. Access requests

We respond to requests from individuals to access their data within one month of receipt, and we keep a record of each request and response.
