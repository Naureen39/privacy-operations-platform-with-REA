# Privacy notice: Web shop

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

Web shop is operated by Northbridge Group. Online store selling clothing and home goods to consumers in five countries. The system owner is Priya Nair, Head of E-commerce.

## Personal data we collect

- your account password (stored only as a hash)
- your e-mail address
- your name
- your telephone number
- your delivery and billing address
- your IP address
- your marketing preferences
- the items you order and the order total
- your payment card details (entered directly with our payment provider)
- a shopping session cookie

## Why we use your data

- Processing and delivering your orders
- Managing your customer account
- Preventing fraud

Our legal basis is the performance of a contract with you.

## Who we share it with

We do not share your personal data with any third party other than our payment provider and our delivery carrier, who process it only to complete your order.

- Stripe (US): payment processing
- SwiftParcel Logistics (GB): parcel delivery
- Google Fonts (US): web font delivery

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for seven years because tax law requires it. Account data is kept until you ask us to delete the account.

## International transfers

Payments are processed by Stripe in the United States under standard contractual clauses.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
