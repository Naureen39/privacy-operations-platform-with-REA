# Privacy notice: Newsletter service

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

Newsletter service is operated by Northbridge Group. Subscription page and list management for the marketing newsletter. The system owner is Daniel Okoye, Digital Marketing Manager.

## Personal data we collect

- your first name
- the topics you are interested in
- your consent and when you gave it
- your postcode
- your IP address when you subscribe
- your e-mail address

## Why we use your data

- Sending you our newsletter

Our legal basis is your consent.

## Who we share it with

We use MailSend Europe to deliver the newsletter. We do not share your data with anyone else.

- MailSend Europe (IE): newsletter delivery

## How long we keep it

We keep your subscription until you unsubscribe, then delete it within 30 days.

## International transfers

Newsletter data stays in the European Economic Area.

## How we handle your data

- We only send marketing e-mails if you actively opt in. No box is ticked for you.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
