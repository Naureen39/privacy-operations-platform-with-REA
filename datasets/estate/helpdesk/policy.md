# Privacy notice: Helpdesk and chat widget

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

Helpdesk and chat widget is operated by Northbridge Group. Customer support tickets and live chat for the shop. The system owner is Imogen Clarke, Customer Care Lead.

## Personal data we collect

- your browser and device type
- your e-mail address
- your name
- your telephone number
- your IP address
- the messages you send us
- the subject of your request

## Why we use your data

- Answering your support requests
- Improving our customer service

Our legal basis is our legitimate interests.

## Who we share it with

We use ChatRelay to translate messages and MailSend Europe to send notification e-mails. Both act only on our instructions.

- ChatRelay (DE): message translation
- MailSend Europe (IE): ticket notification e-mails

## How long we keep it

Support conversations are deleted twelve months after they are closed.

## International transfers

All support data is processed inside the European Economic Area.

## How we handle your data

- Chat transcripts are not stored after the conversation ends.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
