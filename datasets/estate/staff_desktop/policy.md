# Privacy notice: Staff desktop client

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

Staff desktop client is operated by Northbridge Group. Desktop client for staff to view their profile, clock in and out, and submit timesheets. The system owner is Nadia Rahman, IT Manager.

## Personal data we collect

- your location when you clock in or out
- your department and manager
- an identifier of your computer
- your employee number
- your name
- your working hours
- the IP address of your last sign in
- your work telephone number
- your work e-mail address

## Why we use your data

- Recording working time
- Running payroll
- Showing you your own staff profile

Our legal basis is a legal obligation that applies to us.

## Who we share it with

Working time data is passed to our HR system. We do not share it with anyone outside Northbridge.

## How long we keep it

Timesheets are kept for six years.

## International transfers

All data is processed inside the European Economic Area.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
