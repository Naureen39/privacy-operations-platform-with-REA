# Privacy notice: HR onboarding form

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

HR onboarding form is operated by Northbridge Group. Onboarding form completed by new employees before their start date. The system owner is Grace Lindqvist, HR Director.

## Personal data we collect

- your bank account number for salary payments
- your date of birth
- whether you have a disability that needs workplace adjustments
- the name and phone number of your emergency contact
- your name
- your ethnic origin (equality monitoring, optional)
- your home address
- your national identification number
- your nationality and right to work
- your personal e-mail address
- your telephone number
- whether you are a trade union member (for dues deduction)

## Why we use your data

- Setting up your employment record
- Paying your salary
- Meeting our legal duties as an employer

Our legal basis is the performance of a contract with you.

## Who we share it with

We share payroll data with PayrollHub and identity data with VerifyCheck. Both act only on our instructions.

- PayrollHub (PL): payroll processing
- VerifyCheck (GB): pre-employment identity check

## How long we keep it

Employment records are kept for the duration of employment plus six years.

## International transfers

PayrollHub processes data in Poland and VerifyCheck in the United Kingdom.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
