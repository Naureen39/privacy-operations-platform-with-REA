# Privacy notice: CRM

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

CRM is operated by Northbridge Group. Business customers, leads and sales opportunities. The system owner is Marcus Weber, Head of Sales.

## Personal data we collect

- your employer
- seniority and company size added from business data sources
- your job title
- your business e-mail address
- your name
- your business telephone number
- how you found us
- notes our sales team records about you

## Why we use your data

- Responding to quote requests
- Managing business customer relationships

Our legal basis is our legitimate interests.

## Who we share it with

We use MailSend Europe to send confirmation e-mails and LeadScope Data to complete business contact details.

- LeadScope Data (IN): lead enrichment
- MailSend Europe (IE): quote confirmation e-mails

## How long we keep it

Lead and customer records are kept while the business relationship lasts.

## International transfers

LeadScope Data processes business contact data in India.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
