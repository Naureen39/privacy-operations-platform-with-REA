# Privacy notice: Web analytics

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

Web analytics is operated by Northbridge Group. Self hosted analytics for the shop, the newsletter site and the blog. The system owner is Daniel Okoye, Digital Marketing Manager.

## Personal data we collect

- your IP address, shortened before it is stored
- your approximate location (country level)
- the pages you view
- the page that referred you to us
- your browser and operating system
- a random visitor identifier stored in a cookie

## Why we use your data

- Understanding how the site is used
- Improving page performance

Our legal basis is our legitimate interests.

## Who we share it with

Analytics data is processed on our own servers. Aggregated reports are shown to our reporting agency BrightLens, which acts as our service provider.

- BrightLens Agency (GB): marketing reporting dashboards

## How long we keep it

Visit records are kept for 25 months.

## International transfers

Analytics data stays in the European Economic Area. Our reporting agency is based in the United Kingdom.

## How we handle your data

- We anonymise your IP address by removing the last two bytes before anything is stored, so we never store a full IP address.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
