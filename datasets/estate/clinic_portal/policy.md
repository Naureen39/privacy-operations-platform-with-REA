# Privacy notice: Clinic appointment portal

_Reference estate document for the fictional company Northbridge Group. Demonstration use only. Draft for review by a qualified privacy professional. Not legal advice._

## Who we are

Clinic appointment portal is operated by Northbridge Group. Appointment booking for the Northbridge Health clinic arm. The system owner is Dr. Helena Voss, Clinic Director.

## Personal data we collect

- the date and time of your appointment
- your date of birth
- diagnoses recorded by your doctor
- your insurance number
- your insurance provider
- your IP address
- your e-mail address
- your name
- your telephone number
- your reason for visiting
- the symptoms you describe

## Why we use your data

- Booking and managing your appointments
- Sending appointment reminders

Our legal basis is our legitimate interests.

## Who we share it with

We use BookWell Scheduling to reserve appointment slots and SMSGate to send reminders by text message.

- BookWell Scheduling (NL): appointment slot booking
- SMSGate (IN): appointment reminder text messages

## How long we keep it

Appointment records are kept for ten years.

## International transfers

BookWell is based in the Netherlands. SMSGate sends reminders from India.

## Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures that are appropriate to the risk.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Write to privacy@northbridge.example. We answer within one month.
