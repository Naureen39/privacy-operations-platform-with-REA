# Image and asset attribution

The public site uses no third party photographs. Every illustration is original work in this repository:

- The logo, favicon set, Open Graph image, pipeline diagram, industry tiles and product illustrations are drawn as SVG or markup in `src/site/art/` and `public/` (see `npm run images` for the raster versions).
- No company logos, customer names or testimonials appear anywhere on the site. The case cards are labeled sample scenarios.

Third party assets that ship with the build:

| Asset                                                    | Source                     | License                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Inter typeface (self hosted through `@fontsource/inter`) | https://rsms.me/inter/     | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| Icons                                                    | Lucide, https://lucide.dev | ISC License               |

Product screenshots will be generated from the running seeded console with Playwright once the console exists. Until then the site shows clearly labeled illustrations.
